01 — Why Mauritius

Six reasons the island works for Indian students.

Small, safe and easy to settle into, with universities that are regulated, English-medium and closely tied to universities abroad. Hover or tap a pebble.

01EN · FR

English in class, French on the street

Programmes are taught in English. Around you, French and Kreol, a bilingual edge employers notice.

0226 / 41

A foreign degree, taught locally

26 of the 41 institutions teach toward an overseas partner's award: British, Australian, French or Indian.

03IST −1½

Close to home

Direct flights from Indian metros, and the clock runs just 1½ hours behind IST, so calls home stay easy.

04DIWALI

A culture you'll recognise

Most Mauritians have Indian roots. Diwali is a public holiday, and you'll find home food on every corner.

0520 HRS

Work while you study

Up to 20 hours a week once you're authorised. We handle the written request through your institution.

063 YRS

Stay on after graduating

The Young Professional Occupation Permit gives graduates in six priority sectors up to 3 years, from Rs 25,000 a month.

    Land and redo your medicals

    You arrive on a 3-month provisional entry permit. Medical tests must be repeated in Mauritius within one month, or the permit is cancelled.

    We help you make the written request.

    Graduate and stay on

    Apply for the Young Professional Occupation Permit, open to holders of an HEC-recognised degree in six priority sectors.

    We show you the permit route before you choose.

    01

We show you exactly whose qualification you'd earn before you choose, and the Young Professional Occupation Permit later requires a degree recognised by the HEC.

    02/ 05

    Can I bring my spouse or children on a student visa?

    Tap to flip
    02

    No. The Student Visa guidelines make no dependant provision. Dependant permits are tied to the Occupation Permit regime, and even then a dependant permit carries no right to work.

    03/ 05

    Can I work part-time?

    Tap to flip
    03

    Up to 20 hours a week, but only once you're authorised. You make a written request through your institution, which goes to the Ministry for approval. Don't budget on earnings before that approval arrives.
