Why Mauritius

An island that takes your degree seriously.

Mauritius is small, safe and very easy to settle into — and its higher-education sector is regulated, English-medium and closely tied to universities abroad.

English in class, French on the street

Programmes are taught in English. Around you, French and Kreol — a bilingual edge employers notice.

A foreign degree, taught locally

Close to home

A culture you'll recognise

Messages in a bottle

Four things to know before you fly.

The details that quietly sink applications. We'd rather you hear them from us now.

No HEC registration, no visa

Only institutions on the Higher Education Commission's register can host a student visa — and the same check returns when you apply for a post-study permit.

Medicals, again, within a month

Tests done at home aren't the end of it. Repeat them in Mauritius within one month of arrival or your provisional permit is cancelled.

Part-time work isn't automatic

The 20 hours a week needs written authorisation, routed through your institution to the Ministry. Don't count on that income until it's approved.

Families can't come on a student visa

There's no dependant provision for students. Dependant permits sit under the Occupation Permit — and carry no right to work.

Questions from the shore

Frequently asked

Will my Mauritian degree be recognised?

That depends on who awards it. Some institutions award their own degrees; 26 of the 41 teach toward a partner university's award from the UK, Australia, France or India. We show you exactly whose qualification you'd earn before you choose — and the Young Professional Occupation Permit later requires a degree recognised by the HEC.

Can I bring my spouse or children on a student visa?

No. The Student Visa guidelines make no dependant provision. Dependant permits are tied to the Occupation Permit regime — and even then a dependant permit carries no right to work.

Can I work part-time?

Up to 20 hours a week, but only once you're authorised. You make a written request through your institution, which goes to the Ministry for approval. Please don't budget on earnings before that approval arrives.

What happens after I land?

You enter on a 3-month provisional permit. You must repeat your medical tests in Mauritius within one month of arrival, or the permit is cancelled. We send you a checklist and reminders so this one isn't missed.
